# 🌍 Bader & Sahem | بادر و ساهم

**Non-Profit Organization | Established 2016 | Jordan**

> نتطوع لمستقبل أفضل | We Volunteer for a Better Future

---

## ✨ About Us
**Bader & Sahem** is a Jordanian non-profit organization dedicated to:
- Designing and managing **community projects**.
- Promoting a culture of **volunteering**.
- Empowering **youth and students** through guidance and capacity building.
- Partnering with institutions, NGOs, and government entities to maximize impact.

---

## 📊 Our Impact
- **11+ Years** of community experience  
- **40+ Projects & Campaigns**  
- **12,000+ Volunteers** engaged  
- **90+ Partners** across Jordan and the region  
- **70,000+ Beneficiaries** reached  

---

## 🚀 Our Initiatives
- 🎓 [**عينك على المستقبل | Eye on the Future**](https://eyeonfuture.org)  
  Helping students explore majors, universities, and career paths.  

- 💡 [**NextStep**](https://nextstepjo.com) | [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/nextstep.jo/)  
  Youth-led platform for dialogues, workshops, and networking.  

- 🌱 [**Shabab+**](https://shabab.plus) | [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/shabab_plus/)  
  Supporting entrepreneurs and changemakers to transform ideas into impactful ventures.  

- 🤝 **Jordan Volunteering Programs**  
  Structured volunteering opportunities, including *Jordanian Volunteering Days* in partnership with the Ministry of Youth & Naua.
